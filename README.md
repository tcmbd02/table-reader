# Table Reader

Table Reader turns scanned or photographed tables (time cards, attendance sheets, reports) into tables you can check
and download, and prepares the month-end file for Million Payroll. It never guesses: anything it cannot read is left
empty and highlighted for you to check.

This branch holds only what you need to install and use it.

## What you need

- A Windows 10 or 11 PC.
- **Claude Code**, installed and signed in with your own Claude plan (https://claude.com/claude-code). Table Reader
  uses it to read the pictures. Your documents stay on your PC.

## Install

1. Download **`Table Reader.zip`** from this page (click the file, then the download button).
2. Right-click the zip, choose **Extract All**.
3. Open the extracted **Table Reader** folder and double-click **Install Table Reader**. No administrator rights are
   needed. A **Table Reader** shortcut appears on your desktop.
4. Open Table Reader. If it asks, press **Sign in** (top right).

To update, download the new zip and run **Install Table Reader** again. Your documents and payroll are kept.

## Guides

- **`Table Reader guide.pdf`**: adding files, checking the highlighted cells, downloading the table.
- **`GUIDE - Table Reader to Million.html`**: from time cards to a checked import in Million Payroll, step by step
  (download it and open it in your browser).

## Where your work is kept

In `Documents\Table Reader` on your PC: the files you added, your corrections and the payroll months. Nothing is
uploaded by Table Reader itself.
